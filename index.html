About This Platform

Built by a clinician,
for clinicians.

This platform exists to document clinical reasoning in neurological physiotherapy — not as finished answers, but as honest reflections from daily practice.

Every case shared here has been anonymized. The goal is not to present exemplary outcomes, but to think out loud about the complexity of rehabilitation decisions.
